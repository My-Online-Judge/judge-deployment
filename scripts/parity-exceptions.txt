# Differences scripts/parity-run.sh accepts although the verdict is not the one corpus.json expects, one per line:
#   <lang>/<file> <got>-><expected>      (`got`: what the stack answers; `expected`: what the corpus says)
# A line whose `got` is REFUSED applies in every run. Every other line applies only with `--baseline qduoj`: the corpus describes the
# Go sandbox, so against the Go sandbox such a line would hide a regression. Spec docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-go-judge-server-design.md 4.3.
#
# Programs submission-service's source policy refuses before they are queued (HTTP 400, the same whatever sandbox is behind it), so
# they never reach a sandbox through the gateway; judge-server's in-image tests (scripts/run-all.sh) are what judge them. Three of them
# include <linux/futex.h>: rtle and rtle_busy (REAL_TLE) and tle_wait (a CPU spin, then a wait: CPU_TLE). So the corpus produces no
# REAL_TLE verdict through the gateway.
c/socket.c REFUSED->RE
cpp/rtle.cpp REFUSED->REAL_TLE
cpp/rtle_busy.cpp REFUSED->REAL_TLE
cpp/tle_wait.cpp REFUSED->CPU_TLE
java/exec.java REFUSED->RE
java/socket.java REFUSED->RE
python3/fork.py REFUSED->RE
#
# The two intended divergences of the Go sandbox from qduoj (spec 4.3): qduoj answers WA, the Go sandbox AC, as the corpus expects.
cpp/crlf.cpp WA->AC
cpp/stderr.cpp WA->AC
